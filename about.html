We grew up between places.

This is a run club for people who did too — less about pace, more about finding your people on a Saturday morning.

Felix

from Germany, Shanghai, Hong Kong
based New York

Alex

from Germany, Shanghai, London
based New York

We built this because we knew the feeling.

We’re German, but we grew up in China — 14 years in Shanghai. That’s where we became Third Culture Kids. Third Culture Kids find each other fast. You don’t have to explain where you’re from or why you can’t give a single answer to that question. People just get it.

That in-between feeling — belonging everywhere and nowhere at once — is what TCK Run Club is built on. Every Saturday on the West Side Highway, we show up with a group of people who grew up in the same in-between. The conversation picks up fast. It always does.
